NMAP-GUI – PRIVACY STATEMENT
============================

Applies to: nmap-gui 1.0.0 and later
Last updated: 17 November 2026


SUMMARY
-------

nmap-gui runs entirely on your own computer. It does not collect or send any
data about you, has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no update
checks and no tracking. The only network traffic it causes is the scans that
you start yourself. Everything it stores stays in your own home folder, and
you can delete it at any time.

2. NETWORK CONNECTIONS
----------------------

nmap-gui is made of two parts that both run on your computer: a small local
server and the page you see in your web browser.

  - The local server only listens on 127.0.0.1 (your own computer). Other
    computers on your network or on the internet cannot reach it. It also
    refuses requests that come from other websites open in your browser.

  - The page loads nothing from the internet: no fonts, no scripts, no
    images and no analytics from other websites.

  - When you start a scan, nmap-gui runs the program "nmap" on your computer.
    nmap then sends network packets to the targets YOU entered, which is the
    purpose of a network scan. Those devices (and their owners, firewalls or
    network administrators) can see that they were scanned from your
    computer. Only scan networks and devices you own or have permission to
    scan.

  - If you scan a name such as scanme.nmap.org, your computer looks up that
    name with your normal DNS server, as any program would. nmap may also
    look up the names of the addresses it finds.

nmap-gui does NOT:

  - send usage statistics, crash reports or "telemetry",
  - check for updates,
  - upload or share your scan results,
  - send email by itself (see section 4).


3. WHAT THE PROGRAM STORES, AND WHERE
-------------------------------------

Everything is stored only in your own home folder:

  ~/.local/share/nmap-gui/scans/
      One pair of files for every scan in your history:
        <id>.json  the target, scan type, date and time, nmap's output and
                   the results;
        <id>.xml   nmap's own XML report.
      The results describe the scanned devices: IP addresses, MAC addresses
      and network-card makers, hostnames, open ports, the software running on
      them, operating-system guesses and script output (for example web page
      titles or security certificates). Treat these files as you would any
      other information about your network.

  ~/.local/share/nmap-gui/settings.json
      Your email template: the subject, the message wording and the default
      recipients' email addresses.

  Your web browser
      Only your light/dark theme choice, in the browser's local storage for
      the nmap-gui page.

  ~/.local/share/nmap-gui/app/ and installed-files.txt
      The program itself, when installed with install.sh (no personal data).

If you start nmap-gui with sudo (admin mode), it runs as the administrator,
and that session's history and settings are stored in the administrator's
home folder (/root/.local/share/nmap-gui/) instead.

Nothing is shared with other users of the computer beyond what the normal
file permissions of your home folder allow.


4. EMAIL NOTES AND REPORTS
--------------------------

The Changes tab can prepare a note about what changed on your network.

  - "Send email" asks your computer to open your own email program with the
    recipients, subject and text filled in. nmap-gui does not send the email
    and does not see whether it was sent. Once you send it, the people you
    chose receive the information in the note, and your email provider
    handles it under its own privacy terms.

  - "Copy text" puts the note on your clipboard.

  - "Download report" saves an HTML file with the changes to your browser's
    download folder. It stays there until you delete it.


5. HOW TO DELETE YOUR DATA
--------------------------

  - In the program: open a scan and press the bin button to delete it from
    the history (both of its files are removed).
  - Delete the folder ~/.local/share/nmap-gui/scans/ to remove all history,
    and ~/.local/share/nmap-gui/settings.json to reset the email template.
    nmap-gui creates new, empty ones when needed.
  - When uninstalling: "./uninstall.sh --purge" (installer version) removes
    the program and all history and settings. With the RPM package, remove
    the program with "sudo dnf remove nmap-gui" and delete
    ~/.local/share/nmap-gui/ yourself.
  - Downloaded reports are ordinary files in your download folder.


6. CHILDREN
-----------

nmap-gui collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
