The first public release of **nmap-gui**, a modern browser interface for the nmap network scanner.

- Ten ready-made scan types: Find devices, Quick scan, Quick scan without ping, Regular scan, Service

NMAP-GUI – PRIVACY STATEMENT
============================

Applies to: nmap-gui 1.0.0 and later
Last updated: 17 November 2026


SUMMARY
-------

nmap-gui runs entirely on your own computer. It does not collect or send any
data about you, has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no update
checks and no tracking. The only network traffic it causes is the scans that
you start yourself. Everything it stores stays in your own home folder, and
you can delete it at any time.


1. WHO IS RESPONSIBLE
---------------------

nmap-gui is free software, published under the GNU General Public License
version 3 or later by its developer (GitHub: agent-denis). Because the program
sends no data to the developer or anyone else, the developer receives no
information about you, your network or how you use the program.

Questions about this statement:
https://github.com/agent-denis/nmap-gui/issues


2. NETWORK CONNECTIONS
----------------------

nmap-gui is made of two parts that both run on your computer: a small local
server and the page you see in your web browser.

  - The local server only listens on 127.0.0.1 (your own computer). Other
    computers on your network or on the internet cannot reach it. It also
    refuses requests that come from other websites open in your browser.

  - The page loads nothing from the internet: no fonts, no scripts, no
    images and no analytics from other websites.

  - When you start a scan, nmap-gui runs the program "nmap" on your computer.
    nmap then sends network packets to the targets YOU entered, which is the
    purpose of a network scan. Those devices (and their owners, firewalls or
    network administrators) can see that they were scanned from your
    computer. Only scan networks and devices you own or have permission to
    scan.

  - If you scan a name such as scanme.nmap.org, your computer looks up that
    name with your normal DNS server, as any program would. nmap may also
    look up the names of the addresses it finds.

nmap-gui does NOT:

  - send usage statistics, crash reports or "telemetry",
  - check for updates,
  - upload or share your scan results,
  - send email by itself (see section 4).


3. WHAT THE PROGRAM STORES, AND WHERE
-------------------------------------

Everything is stored only in your own home folder:

  ~/.local/share/nmap-gui/scans/
      One pair of files for every scan in your history:
        <id>.json  the target, scan type, date and time, nmap's output and
                   the results;
        <id>.xml   nmap's own XML report.
      The results describe the scanned devices: IP addresses, MAC addresses
      and network-card makers, hostnames, open ports, the software running on
      them, operating-system guesses and script output (for example web page
      titles or security certificates). Treat these files as you would any
      other information about your network.

  ~/.local/share/nmap-gui/settings.json
      Your email template: the subject, the message wording and the default
      recipients' email addresses.

  Your web browser
      Only your light/dark theme choice, in the browser's local storage for
      the nmap-gui page.

  ~/.local/share/nmap-gui/app/ and installed-files.txt
      The program itself, when installed with install.sh (no personal data).

If you start nmap-gui with sudo (admin mode), it runs as the administrator,
and that session's history and settings are stored in the administrator's
home folder (/root/.local/share/nmap-gui/) instead.

Nothing is shared with other users of the computer beyond what the normal
file permissions of your home folder allow.


4. EMAIL NOTES AND REPORTS
--------------------------

The Changes tab can prepare a note about what changed on your network.

  - "Send email" asks your computer to open your own email program with the
    recipients, subject and text filled in. nmap-gui does not send the email
    and does not see whether it was sent. Once you send it, the people you
    chose receive the information in the note, and your email provider
    handles it under its own privacy terms.

  - "Copy text" puts the note on your clipboard.

  - "Download report" saves an HTML file with the changes to your browser's
    download folder. It stays there until you delete it.


5. HOW TO DELETE YOUR DATA
--------------------------

  - In the program: open a scan and press the bin button to delete it from
    the history (both of its files are removed).
  - Delete the folder ~/.local/share/nmap-gui/scans/ to remove all history,
    and ~/.local/share/nmap-gui/settings.json to reset the email template.
    nmap-gui creates new, empty ones when needed.
  - When uninstalling: "./uninstall.sh --purge" (installer version) removes
    the program and all history and settings. With the RPM package, remove
    the program with "sudo dnf remove nmap-gui" and delete
    ~/.local/share/nmap-gui/ yourself.
  - Downloaded reports are ordinary files in your download folder.


6. CHILDREN
-----------

nmap-gui collects no personal information from anyone, including children.


7. CHANGES TO THIS STATEMENT
----------------------------

If a future version changes how data is handled, this statement will be
updated and the change will be listed in CHANGELOG.md. The current version is
always in the source code: PRIVACY.txt.

# nmap-gui

**A modern, easy browser interface for the nmap network scanner.**
Pick a target, pick a scan type, press Start, and see which devices are on your network, which ports
are open and what changed since last time.

![nmap-gui: choose a target and one of ten ready-made scan types](docs/screenshots/new-scan.png)

nmap-gui runs a small program on your own computer and shows its interface in your web browser.
It starts [nmap](https://nmap.org) for you, turns nmap's results into tidy cards, tables and a network
map, keeps a history of every scan, and can tell you what changed between two scans, with a
ready-to-send email note for the people who need to know.

> Only scan networks and computers that you own or have permission to scan.

## Features

- **Ten ready-made scan types**, from a quick device search to service versions, scripts, OS
  detection and UDP. The exact nmap command is shown before you start.
- **Live progress:** devices and open ports appear while the scan runs; stop it at any time.
- **Clear results:** a card for every device (hostname, MAC address, vendor, OS guess, open ports and
  software versions), a port table you can filter, and nmap's raw output.
- **Network map:** every device around the scanner, coloured by how many ports are open.
  Click a device to see its details.
- **History:** every scan is saved, and stays after you close the program.
- **Changes:** compare a scan with an earlier one: new devices, devices not seen, ports that opened or
  closed, changed software, a device that moved to another IP address, or a different device on a known
  address.
- **Email note:** a ready-to-send message about the changes, from your own template, with
  *Send email*, *Copy text* and *Download report*.
- **Light and dark theme**, works in narrow windows too.
- **No extra packages:** only Python 3 and nmap.

![Results in the dark theme: one card per device](docs/screenshots/results-dark.png)

## Install

nmap-gui needs **Python 3.8 or newer** and **nmap** (`sudo dnf install nmap` on Fedora,
`sudo apt install nmap` on Debian/Ubuntu).

### Fedora (RPM)

Download `nmap-gui-1.0.0-1.fc45.noarch.rpm` from the
[Releases page](https://github.com/agent-denis/nmap-gui/releases) and install it:

```sh
sudo dnf install ./nmap-gui-1.0.0-1.fc45.noarch.rpm
```

The same RPM works on Fedora 44, 45 and newer. dnf installs nmap too if it is missing.

### Any Linux, for your user only

```sh
git clone https://github.com/agent-denis/nmap-gui.git
cd nmap-gui
./install.sh
```

`install.sh` needs no root rights. It copies nmap-gui to `~/.local/share/nmap-gui/app`, adds the
`nmap-gui` command to `~/.local/bin` and adds nmap-gui to your app menu. Run it again to upgrade.
Remove it with `./uninstall.sh` (add `--purge` to also delete your scan history and settings).

### Without installing

```sh
./nmap-gui                 # opens http://127.0.0.1:8642 in your browser; Ctrl+C stops it
```

## Using nmap-gui

Open **nmap-gui** from your app menu. It starts in the background and opens in your browser. Clicking
the icon again just opens the page again.

1. Type a **target**: one address (`192.168.1.10`), a whole home network (`192.168.1.0/24`), a range
   (`10.0.0.1-50`) or a name (`scanme.nmap.org`, nmap's own test server).
2. Pick a **scan type** and press **Start scan**.
3. Watch the results arrive. The tabs show **Hosts**, **Ports**, **Network map**, **Changes** and
   nmap's **Raw output**.

nmap-gui stops by itself 10 minutes after you close the last page (unless a scan is running). You can
also stop it with the ⏻ button on the page, with *Stop nmap-gui* in the icon's right-click menu, or with
`nmap-gui --stop`.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `nmap-gui` | Start and open the browser |
| `nmap-gui --port 9000` | Use another port (default 8642) |
| `nmap-gui --no-browser` | Don't open the browser |
| `nmap-gui --stop` | Stop a running nmap-gui |
| `sudo nmap-gui` | Admin mode: unlocks OS detection, UDP and Intense scans |

![Network map: devices around the scanner, coloured by open ports](docs/screenshots/network-map.png)

## Scan types

| Scan type | nmap options | Needs sudo |
|---|---|---|
| Find devices | `-sn` | |
| Quick scan | `-T4 -F` | |
| Quick scan, no ping | `-T4 -F -Pn` | |
| Regular scan | (nmap's defaults) | |
| Service versions | `-T4 -sV` | |
| Versions + safe scripts | `-T4 -sV -sC` | |
| All TCP ports | `-T4 -p-` | |
| OS detection | `-T4 -O --osscan-guess` | yes |
| UDP scan | `-T4 -sU --top-ports 100` | yes |
| Intense scan | `-T4 -A` | yes |

nmap-gui always adds `-v` (to show progress) and saves nmap's XML report. Scan types that need sudo are
greyed out unless nmap-gui was started with `sudo nmap-gui`.

## Comparing scans and the email note

Open a scan and choose the **Changes** tab. nmap-gui compares it with the previous scan of the same
target (or any scan you pick) and lists what changed.

Below the list is an **email note**, filled in from a template:

> We noticed differences on 192.168.1.0/24 between the scan on Mon, 16 Nov 2026, 13:32 and the scan
> on Tue, 17 Nov 2026, 13:02. Summary: 2 new devices, 1 device not seen, port changes on 1 device. …

- **Send email** opens your email program with recipient, subject and text filled in; you press Send
  there. nmap-gui never sends email itself.
- **Copy text** copies the note, for webmail.
- **Download report** saves a tidy HTML report to attach.
- **Edit template** changes the wording and the default recipients. Words in curly brackets such as
  `{new_devices}` or `{old_time}` are filled in automatically.

![Changes tab: new devices, devices not seen and port changes, with the email note](docs/screenshots/changes.png)

## Privacy and safety

- nmap-gui collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. It has no accounts, no analytics and no update
  checks. The only network traffic is the scans that you start. See [PRIVACY.txt](PRIVACY.txt).
- The interface is only reachable from your own computer (127.0.0.1). Requests from other websites are
  refused.
- Targets are checked and passed straight to nmap, never through a shell, so they can't be turned into
  extra nmap options or commands.
- Scan history (IP addresses, MAC addresses, hostnames, open ports) is stored in your home folder in
  `~/.local/share/nmap-gui/`. Delete it at any time.

## For developers

nmap-gui is plain Python (standard library only), HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no build step.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `server.py` | Local web server: runs nmap, reads its XML, compares scans, saves history |
| `static/index.html`, `app.css` | Page layout and design (light/dark) |
| `static/app.js` | Scans, results, map, history |
| `static/compare.js` | Changes tab and email note |
| `tests/` | End-to-end test (scans 127.0.0.1 only) and comparison tests |
| `check.sh` | Runs all tests |
| `install.sh`, `uninstall.sh` | Per-user install with app menu entry |
| `packaging/` | Desktop entry, AppStream metadata, icons, RPM spec and `build-rpm.sh` |
| `tools/export_text.py` | Makes `README.txt` and `nmap-gui-source.txt` |

Run the tests with `./check.sh`, and build the RPM with `./packaging/build-rpm.sh`.

#!/usr/bin/env bash
# Copyright (C) 2026 Denis (https://github.com/agent-denis)
# SPDX-License-Identifier: GPL-3.0-or-later
# Install nmap-gui for the current user (no root, no sudo needed).
#
#   ./install.sh        install, or upgrade an existing install (scan history and settings are kept)
#
# Puts everything under your home folder:
#   ~/.local/share/nmap-gui/app        the program
#   ~/.local/bin/nmap-gui              the command
#   ~/.local/share/applications/...    app menu entry, plus icons and AppStream metadata
# Undo with ./uninstall.sh
set -euo pipefail

APP_ID=io.github.agent_denis.NmapGui
say()  { printf '  %s\n' "$*"; }
step() { printf '\n>> %s\n' "$*"; }
die()  { printf '\nERROR: %s\n' "$*" >&2; exit 1; }

[ "$(id -u)" -ne 0 ] || die "Don't run the installer as root. nmap-gui installs for your own user only."

SRC="$(cd "$(dirname "${BASH_SOURCE[0]}")" && pwd)"
[ -f "$SRC/server.py" ] && [ -d "$SRC/static" ] || die "Run install.sh from the nmap-gui folder."
VERSION="$(sed -n 's/^__version__ = "\(.*\)"/\1/p' "$SRC/server.py")"

DATA="${XDG_DATA_HOME:-$HOME/.local/share}"
case "$DATA" in /*) ;; *) DATA="$HOME/.local/share" ;; esac
BIN="$HOME/.local/bin"
APPDIR="$DATA/nmap-gui/app"
MANIFEST="$DATA/nmap-gui/installed-files.txt"

echo "nmap-gui $VERSION - installer"

step "Checking what nmap-gui needs"
command -v python3 >/dev/null 2>&1 || die "python3 not found. Install it with: sudo dnf install python3"
python3 -c 'import sys; sys.exit(0 if sys.version_info >= (3, 8) else 1)' || die "Python 3.8 or newer is needed."
say "Python $(python3 -c 'import platform; print(platform.python_version())')"
if command -v nmap >/dev/null 2>&1; then
    say "$(nmap --version | head -1)"
else

def parse_targets(text):
    parts = str(text).split()
    if not parts:
        raise ValueError("Enter a target, e.g. 192.168.1.0/24 or scanme.nmap.org")
    if len(parts) > 32:
        raise ValueError("Too many targets (max 32)")
    for p in parts:
        if not TARGET_RE.match(p):
            raise ValueError(f"Not a valid target: {p}")
    return parts

def nmap_version():
    """Asks nmap for its version once, then remembers the answer."""
    if not _VERSION:
        _VERSION.append(_read_nmap_version())
    return _VERSION[0]


def _read_nmap_version():
    if not NMAP:
        return None
    try:
        out = subprocess.run([NMAP, "--version"], capture_output=True, text=True, timeout=10).stdout
        m = re.search(r"Nmap version (\S+)", out)
        return m.group(1) if m else "unknown"
    except (OSError, subprocess.SubprocessError):
        return None


# ---------------------------------------------------------------- XML parsing

def _attr(el, name, default=""):
    return el.get(name, default) if el is not None else default


def parse_xml(path):
    """Turn nmap's XML report into simple JSON-friendly data."""
    root = ET.parse(path).getroot()
    hosts = []
    for h in root.findall("host"):
        if _attr(h.find("status"), "state") != "up":
            continue
        ipv4 = ipv6 = mac = vendor = ""
        for a in h.findall("address"):
            t = a.get("addrtype")
            if t == "ipv4":
                ipv4 = a.get("addr", "")
            elif t == "ipv6":
                ipv6 = a.get("addr", "")
            elif t == "mac":
                mac, vendor = a.get("addr", ""), a.get("vendor", "")
        ports = []
        for p in h.findall("ports/port"):
            svc = p.find("service")
            product = " ".join(x for x in (_attr(svc, "product"), _attr(svc, "version"),
                                           _attr(svc, "extrainfo")) if x)
            ports.append({
                "port": int(p.get("portid", 0)),
                "proto": p.get("protocol", ""),
                "state": _attr(p.find("state"), "state"),
                "reason": _attr(p.find("state"), "reason"),
                "service": _attr(svc, "name"),
                "product": product,
                "scripts": [{"id": s.get("id", ""), "output": s.get("output", "")}
                            for s in p.findall("script")],
            })
        hidden = [{"state": e.get("state", ""), "count": int(e.get("count", 0))}
                  for e in h.findall("ports/extraports")]
        osm = h.find("os/osmatch")
        hosts.append({
            "ip": ipv4 or ipv6,
            "ipv6": ipv6,
            "mac": mac,
            "vendor": vendor,
            "hostnames": [n.get("name", "") for n in h.findall("hostnames/hostname")],
            "reason": _attr(h.find("status"), "reason"),
            "ports": ports,
            "hidden_ports": hidden,
            "os": {"name": osm.get("name"), "accuracy": osm.get("accuracy")} if osm is not None else None,
            "distance": int(_attr(h.find("distance"), "value", "0") or 0) or None,
            "trace": [{"ttl": hop.get("ttl"), "ip": hop.get("ipaddr"), "host": hop.get("host", "")}
                      for hop in h.findall("trace/hop")],
            "scripts": [{"id": s.get("id", ""), "output": s.get("output", "")}
                        for s in h.findall("hostscript/script")],
        })
    fin = root.find("runstats/finished")
    hs = root.find("runstats/hosts")
    return {
        "hosts": hosts,
        "summary": _attr(fin, "summary"),
        "elapsed": float(_attr(fin, "elapsed", "0") or 0),
        "hosts_up": int(_attr(hs, "up", "0") or 0),
        "hosts_down": int(_attr(hs, "down", "0") or 0),
        "hosts_total": int(_attr(hs, "total", "0") or 0),
    }


# ---------------------------------------------------------------- comparing two scans

def _open_ports(host):
    return {(p["port"], p["proto"]): p for p in host["ports"] if p["state"] == "open"}


def _brief(host):
    return {k: host.get(k) for k in ("ip", "hostnames", "mac", "vendor", "ports", "os")}


def diff_scans(a, b):
    """What changed between two finished scans. The older one is always 'old'."""
    old, new = (a, b) if a.started <= b.started else (b, a)
    oh = {h["ip"]: h for h in (old.result or {}).get("hosts", [])}
    nh = {h["ip"]: h for h in (new.result or {}).get("hosts", [])}
    new_ips = [ip for ip in nh if ip not in oh]
    gone_ips = [ip for ip in oh if ip not in nh]
    other = []

    # Same physical device (same MAC) that just got a different IP is not "new".
    gone_by_mac = {oh[ip]["mac"]: ip for ip in gone_ips if oh[ip].get("mac")}
    for ip in list(new_ips):
        mac = nh[ip].get("mac")
        if mac and mac in gone_by_mac:
            old_ip = gone_by_mac.pop(mac)
            new_ips.remove(ip)
            gone_ips.remove(old_ip)
            other.append({"ip": ip, "hostnames": nh[ip]["hostnames"], "what": "IP address",
                          "old": old_ip, "new": ip})

    ping_only = "ping" in (old.preset["id"], new.preset["id"])
    warnings = []
    if ping_only:
        warnings.append("One of the scans only looked for devices, so open ports were not compared.")
    elif old.preset["id"] != new.preset["id"]:
        warnings.append(f"The scans used different scan types ({old.preset['name']} vs "
                        f"{new.preset['name']}), so some port differences may come from that.")
    for s in (old, new):
        if s.status != "done":
            warnings.append(f"The scan from {time.strftime('%d %b %Y %H:%M', time.localtime(s.started))} "
                            f"did not finish ({s.status}), so its results may be incomplete.")
    if " ".join(old.targets) != " ".join(new.targets):
        warnings.append("The two scans had different targets.")

    port_changes = []
    for ip in nh:
        if ip not in oh:
            continue
        o, n = oh[ip], nh[ip]
        if not ping_only:
            op, np_ = _open_ports(o), _open_ports(n)
            opened = [np_[k] for k in sorted(np_) if k not in op]
            closed = [op[k] for k in sorted(op) if k not in np_]
            changed = [{"port": k[0], "proto": k[1], "old": op[k]["product"], "new": np_[k]["product"]}
                       for k in sorted(np_) if k in op and op[k]["product"] and np_[k]["product"]
                       and op[k]["product"] != np_[k]["product"]]
            if opened or closed or changed:
                port_changes.append({"ip": ip, "hostnames": n["hostnames"], "opened": opened,
                                     "closed": closed, "changed": changed})
        if o.get("mac") and n.get("mac") and o["mac"] != n["mac"]:
            other.append({"ip": ip, "hostnames": n["hostnames"], "what": "MAC address (different device?)",
                          "old": f"{o['mac']} {o.get('vendor', '')}".strip(),
                          "new": f"{n['mac']} {n.get('vendor', '')}".strip()})
        if o["hostnames"] and n["hostnames"] and o["hostnames"] != n["hostnames"]:
            other.append({"ip": ip, "hostnames": n["hostnames"], "what": "Hostname",
                          "old": ", ".join(o["hostnames"]), "new": ", ".join(n["hostnames"])})

    return {
        "old": old.summary(), "new": new.summary(),
        "new_hosts": [_brief(nh[ip]) for ip in new_ips],
        "missing_hosts": [_brief(oh[ip]) for ip in gone_ips],
        "port_changes": port_changes,
        "other_changes": other,
        "ports_compared": not ping_only,
        "warnings": warnings,
    }

def save_settings(data):

    tmp = SETTINGS_FILE.with_suffix(".tmp")
    tmp.write_text(json.dumps(clean, indent=2))
    tmp.replace(SETTINGS_FILE)
    return clean

class Scan:
    def __init__(self, targets, preset):
        self.id = time.strftime("%Y%m%d-%H%M%S-") + uuid.uuid4().hex[:6]
        self.targets = targets
        self.preset = preset
        self.args = ["-v", "--stats-every", "2s", *preset["args"]]
        self.status = "running"          # running | done | failed | cancelled
        self.started = time.time()
        self.finished = None
        self.progress = 0.0
        self.log = []
        self.result = None
        self.error = None
        self.proc = None
        self.cond = threading.Condition()

    @property
    def command(self):
        shown = [a for a in self.args if a not in ("--stats-every", "2s")]
        return " ".join(["nmap", *shown, *self.targets])

    def summary(self):
        r = self.result or {}
        return {
            "id": self.id, "target": " ".join(self.targets), "preset": self.preset["id"],
            "preset_name": self.preset["name"], "command": self.command, "status": self.status,
            "started": self.started, "finished": self.finished, "progress": self.progress,
            "hosts_up": r.get("hosts_up"),
            "open_ports": sum(1 for h in r.get("hosts", []) for p in h["ports"] if p["state"] == "open"),
        }

    def full(self):
        return {**self.summary(), "result": self.result, "error": self.error, "log": self.log}

    def _push(self, line):
        with self.cond:
            self.log.append(line)
            self.cond.notify_all()

    def run(self):
        xml_path = DATA / f"{self.id}.xml"
        cmd = [NMAP, *self.args, "-oX", str(xml_path), *self.targets]
        try:
            self.proc = subprocess.Popen(cmd, stdout=subprocess.PIPE, stderr=subprocess.STDOUT,
                                         text=True, bufsize=1, errors="replace")
            for line in self.proc.stdout:
                line = line.rstrip("\n")
                m = PROGRESS_RE.search(line)
                if m:
                    with self.cond:
                        self.progress = max(self.progress, float(m.group(1)))
                        self.cond.notify_all()
                    continue
                if line.startswith("Stats: "):
                    continue  # periodic timing noise
                self._push(line)
            rc = self.proc.wait()
        except OSError as e:
            rc, self.error = -1, str(e)

        if xml_path.exists():
            try:
                self.result = parse_xml(xml_path)
            except ET.ParseError:
                pass  # cancelled scans leave half-written XML
        with self.cond:
            if self.status == "cancelled":
                pass
            elif rc == 0:
                self.status, self.progress = "done", 100.0
            else:
                self.status = "failed"
                if not self.error:
                    tail = [l for l in self.log if l.strip()][-3:]
                    self.error = " ".join(tail) or f"nmap exited with code {rc}"
            self.finished = time.time()
            self.cond.notify_all()
        self.save()

    def cancel(self):
        with self.cond:
            if self.status != "running":
                return
            self.status = "cancelled"
        if self.proc:
            self.proc.terminate()

    def save(self):
        data = {**self.full(), "targets": self.targets}
        tmp = DATA / f"{self.id}.json.tmp"
        tmp.write_text(json.dumps(data))
        tmp.replace(DATA / f"{self.id}.json")

    @classmethod
    def load(cls, path):
        d = json.loads(path.read_text())
        s = cls.__new__(cls)
        s.id, s.targets = d["id"], d["targets"]
        s.preset = PRESET_BY_ID.get(d["preset"], {"id": d["preset"], "name": d.get("preset_name", ""), "args": []})
        s.args = ["-v", *s.preset["args"]]
        s.status, s.started, s.finished = d["status"], d["started"], d.get("finished")
        s.progress, s.log, s.result, s.error = d.get("progress", 0), d.get("log", []), d.get("result"), d.get("error")
        s.proc, s.cond = None, threading.Condition()
        if s.status == "running":       # server was stopped mid-scan
            s.status, s.error = "failed", "Interrupted (server stopped)"
        return s


class Store:
    def __init__(self):
        DATA.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)
        self.scans = {}
        self.lock = threading.Lock()
        for f in DATA.glob("*.json"):
            try:
                s = Scan.load(f)
                self.scans[s.id] = s
            except (ValueError, KeyError, OSError):
                pass

    def start(self, targets, preset):
        s = Scan(targets, preset)
        with self.lock:
            self.scans[s.id] = s
        threading.Thread(target=s.run, daemon=True).start()
        return s

    def get(self, sid):
        with self.lock:
            return self.scans.get(sid)

    def list(self):
        with self.lock:
            items = list(self.scans.values())
        return [s.summary() for s in sorted(items, key=lambda s: s.started, reverse=True)]

    def running(self):
        with self.lock:
            return [s for s in self.scans.values() if s.status == "running"]

    def cancel_all(self):
        """Stop running scans and give them a moment to save as 'cancelled'."""
        scans = self.running()
        for s in scans:
            s.cancel()
        deadline = time.time() + 3
        while time.time() < deadline and any(s.finished is None for s in scans):
            time.sleep(0.05)

    def delete(self, sid):
        with self.lock:
            s = self.scans.get(sid)
            if not s or s.status == "running":
                return False
            del self.scans[sid]
        for ext in (".json", ".xml"):
            (DATA / f"{sid}{ext}").unlink(missing_ok=True)
        return True

class Handler(BaseHTTPRequestHandler):
    server_version = "nmap-gui"
    store: Store = None
    allowed_hosts: set = set()
    last_seen = time.time()   # last request from a page; used to stop when idle

    def log_message(self, fmt, *args):
        pass  # keep the terminal quiet

    def _send(self, code, body=b"", ctype="application/json"):
        if isinstance(body, (dict, list)):
            body = json.dumps(body).encode()
        self.send_response(code)
        self.send_header("Content-Type", ctype)
        self.send_header("Content-Length", str(len(body)))
        self.send_header("Cache-Control", "no-store")
        self.send_header("X-Content-Type-Options", "nosniff")
        self.end_headers()
        self.wfile.write(body)

    def _guard(self):
        # Blocks other websites from talking to this server (DNS rebinding).
        if self.headers.get("Host", "") not in self.allowed_hosts:
            self._send(403, {"error": "forbidden host"})
            return False
        Handler.last_seen = time.time()
        return True

    def _json_body(self):
        # Requiring a JSON content type means other websites can't silently
        # post to us (browsers block that cross-site without our permission).
        if not self.headers.get("Content-Type", "").startswith("application/json"):
            raise ValueError("expected JSON")
        n = int(self.headers.get("Content-Length", 0) or 0)
        return json.loads(self.rfile.read(min(n, 65536)) or b"{}")

    def do_GET(self):
        if not self._guard():
            return
        path = urlparse(self.path).path
        if path in STATIC_FILES:
            f = STATIC / STATIC_FILES[path]
            return self._send(200, f.read_bytes(), CONTENT_TYPES[f.suffix])
        if path == "/api/info":
            return self._send(200, {"nmap": nmap_version(), "root": IS_ROOT, "presets": PRESETS})
        if path == "/api/scans":
            return self._send(200, self.store.list())
        if path == "/api/settings":
            return self._send(200, {"settings": load_settings(), "defaults": DEFAULT_SETTINGS})
        if path == "/api/compare":
            q = parse_qs(urlparse(self.path).query)
            a = self.store.get(q.get("old", [""])[0])
            b = self.store.get(q.get("new", [""])[0])
            if not a or not b:
                return self._send(404, {"error": "scan not found"})
            if a.id == b.id:
                return self._send(400, {"error": "pick two different scans"})
            if a.status == "running" or b.status == "running" or not a.result or not b.result:
                return self._send(400, {"error": "both scans need results to compare"})
            return self._send(200, diff_scans(a, b))
        m = re.fullmatch(r"/api/scans/([\w-]+)(/events)?", path)
        if m:
            s = self.store.get(m.group(1))
            if not s:
                return self._send(404, {"error": "not found"})
            return self._events(s) if m.group(2) else self._send(200, s.full())
        self._send(404, {"error": "not found"})

    def do_POST(self):
        if not self._guard():
            return
        path = urlparse(self.path).path
        try:
            body = self._json_body()
        except ValueError as e:
            return self._send(400, {"error": str(e)})
        if path == "/api/scans":
            if not NMAP:
                return self._send(500, {"error": "nmap is not installed"})
            preset = PRESET_BY_ID.get(body.get("preset"))
            if not preset:
                return self._send(400, {"error": "Unknown scan type"})
            if preset["root"] and not IS_ROOT:
                return self._send(400, {"error": f"'{preset['name']}' needs administrator rights (sudo)"})
            try:
                targets = parse_targets(body.get("target", ""))
            except ValueError as e:
                return self._send(400, {"error": str(e)})
            return self._send(201, self.store.start(targets, preset).summary())
        if path == "/api/settings":
            try:
                return self._send(200, {"settings": save_settings(body), "defaults": DEFAULT_SETTINGS})
            except ValueError as e:
                return self._send(400, {"error": str(e)})
        if path == "/api/quit":
            self._send(200, {"ok": True})
            self.store.cancel_all()
            threading.Thread(target=self.server.shutdown, daemon=True).start()
            return
        m = re.fullmatch(r"/api/scans/([\w-]+)/cancel", path)
        if m and (s := self.store.get(m.group(1))):
            s.cancel()
            return self._send(200, s.summary())
        self._send(404, {"error": "not found"})

    def do_DELETE(self):
        if not self._guard():
            return
        m = re.fullmatch(r"/api/scans/([\w-]+)", urlparse(self.path).path)
        if m and self.store.delete(m.group(1)):
            return self._send(200, {"ok": True})
        self._send(400, {"error": "cannot delete (missing or still running)"})

    def _events(self, s):
        """Server-sent events: streams new output lines and progress live."""
        self.send_response(200)
        self.send_header("Content-Type", "text/event-stream")
        self.send_header("Cache-Control", "no-store")
        self.end_headers()
        sent, last_progress = 0, -1
        try:
            while True:
                with s.cond:
                    while (sent == len(s.log) and s.progress == last_progress and s.status == "running"):
                        if not s.cond.wait(timeout=15):
                            break
                    lines, status, progress = s.log[sent:], s.status, s.progress
                sent += len(lines)
                msg = ""
                if lines:
                    msg += f"event: log\ndata: {json.dumps(lines)}\n\n"
                if progress != last_progress:
                    last_progress = progress
                    msg += f"event: progress\ndata: {progress}\n\n"
                if status != "running":
                    msg += f"event: end\ndata: {json.dumps(status)}\n\n"
                self.wfile.write((msg or ": ping\n\n").encode())
                self.wfile.flush()
                if status != "running":
                    return
        except (BrokenPipeError, ConnectionResetError):
            return


def _local(port, method, path, body=None):
    """Talk to an nmap-gui that may already be running on this port."""
    c = http.client.HTTPConnection("127.0.0.1", port, timeout=3)
    headers = {"Host": f"127.0.0.1:{port}"}
    if body is not None:
        headers["Content-Type"] = "application/json"
    c.request(method, path, body=body, headers=headers)
    r = c.getresponse()
    return r.status, r.read()


def already_running(port):
    try:
        status, data = _local(port, "GET", "/api/info")
        return status == 200 and "presets" in json.loads(data)
    except (OSError, ValueError):
        return False


def notify(msg):
    """Show a message in the terminal and as a desktop notification."""
    print(msg, file=sys.stderr)
    if shutil.which("notify-send"):
        subprocess.run(["notify-send", "--app-name=nmap-gui", f"--icon={APP_ID}", "nmap-gui", msg],
                       check=False, stdout=subprocess.DEVNULL, stderr=subprocess.DEVNULL)


def idle_watchdog(httpd, minutes):
    while True:
        time.sleep(min(15, minutes * 15))
        if not Handler.store.running() and time.time() - Handler.last_seen > minutes * 60:
            print(f"No page open for {minutes} minutes - stopping.")
            httpd.shutdown()
            return


def main():
    ap = argparse.ArgumentParser(description="Browser interface for nmap")
    ap.add_argument("--port", type=int, default=int(os.environ.get("NMAP_GUI_PORT", DEFAULT_PORT)))
    ap.add_argument("--no-browser", action="store_true", help="don't open the browser automatically")
    ap.add_argument("--app", action="store_true",
                    help=f"desktop-launcher mode: stop by itself after {IDLE_MINUTES} minutes with no page open")
    ap.add_argument("--stop", action="store_true", help="stop an nmap-gui that is already running")
    ap.add_argument("--version", action="version", version=f"nmap-gui {__version__}")
    a = ap.parse_args()
    url = f"http://127.0.0.1:{a.port}/"

    if a.stop:
        try:
            _local(a.port, "POST", "/api/quit", "{}")
            print("nmap-gui stopped.")
        except OSError:
            print("nmap-gui is not running.")
        return

    if already_running(a.port):
        print(f"nmap-gui is already running at {url}")
        if not a.no_browser:
            webbrowser.open(url)
        return

    try:
        httpd = ThreadingHTTPServer(("127.0.0.1", a.port), Handler)
    except OSError as e:
        notify(f"Can't start: port {a.port} is used by another program ({e.strerror}). "
               f"Try: nmap-gui --port {a.port + 1}")
        sys.exit(1)
    Handler.store = Store()
    port = httpd.server_address[1]
    Handler.allowed_hosts = {f"127.0.0.1:{port}", f"localhost:{port}"}
    url = f"http://127.0.0.1:{port}/"
    print(f"nmap-gui {__version__} running at {url}  (press Ctrl+C to stop)")
    if not NMAP:
        notify("nmap was not found. Install it with: sudo dnf install nmap")
    if a.app:
        threading.Thread(target=idle_watchdog, args=(httpd, IDLE_MINUTES), daemon=True).start()
    if not a.no_browser:
        threading.Timer(0.5, webbrowser.open, args=[url]).start()
    try:
        httpd.serve_forever()
    except KeyboardInterrupt:
        print("\nStopped.")
    finally:
        Handler.store.cancel_all()
        httpd.server_close()


if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()

async function api(path, opts = {}) {
  const res = await fetch(path, {
    ...opts,
    headers: opts.body ? { "Content-Type": "application/json" } : {},
  });
  const data = await res.json().catch(() => ({}));
  if (!res.ok) throw new Error(data.error || `Request failed (${res.status})`);
  return data;
}

// ------------------------------------------------------------------ helpers

function timeAgo(ts) {
  const s = Math.round(Date.now() / 1000 - ts);
  if (s < 60) return "just now";
  if (s < 3600) return `${Math.floor(s / 60)} min ago`;
  if (s < 86400) return `${Math.floor(s / 3600)} h ago`;
  return new Date(ts * 1000).toLocaleDateString();
}

function duration(sec) {
  if (sec == null) return "–";
  if (sec < 60) return `${sec.toFixed(1)} s`;
  return `${Math.floor(sec / 60)} min ${Math.round(sec % 60)} s`;
}

function ipKey(ip) {
  // Sort IPv4 numerically; anything else alphabetically after.
  const p = ip.split(".");
  return p.length === 4 && p.every((x) => /^\d+$/.test(x))
    ? p.map((x) => x.padStart(3, "0")).join(".") : "~" + ip;
}

function followLive(s) {
  const es = new EventSource(`/api/scans/${encodeURIComponent(s.id)}/events`);
  state.events = es;
  let pending = false;
  es.addEventListener("log", (e) => {
    s.log.push(...JSON.parse(e.data));
    // Re-render at most a few times a second while output streams in.
    if (!pending) { pending = true; setTimeout(() => { pending = false; if (state.current === s) renderScan(); }, 300); }
  });
  es.addEventListener("progress", (e) => { s.progress = parseFloat(e.data); renderProgress(); });
  es.addEventListener("end", async () => {
    closeEvents();
    await loadHistory();
    if (state.current && state.current.id === s.id) showScan(s.id);
  });
  es.onerror = () => { /* browser retries automatically */ };
}

// While running, nmap's results file isn't ready yet, so build a live
// preview from its text output.
function liveResult(log) {
  const hosts = new Map();
  const get = (ip, name) => {
    if (!hosts.has(ip)) hosts.set(ip, { ip, hostnames: name ? [name] : [], ports: [], hidden_ports: [], trace: [], scripts: [], live: true });
    return hosts.get(ip);
  };
  for (const line of log) {
    let m = line.match(/^Nmap scan report for (\S+)(?: \(([^)]+)\))?(.*)$/);
    if (m && !/host down/.test(m[3])) { m[2] ? get(m[2], m[1]) : get(m[1]); continue; }
    m = line.match(/^Discovered open port (\d+)\/(\w+) on (\S+)/);
    if (m) get(m[3]).ports.push({ port: +m[1], proto: m[2], state: "open", service: "", product: "", scripts: [] });
  }
  const list = [...hosts.values()];
  return { hosts: list, hosts_up: list.length, live: true };
}

function renderHosts(result, running) {
  const panel = $("#tab-hosts");
  const expanded = new Set([...panel.querySelectorAll(".host.expanded")].map((el) => el.dataset.ip));
  const hosts = [...result.hosts].sort((a, b) => ipKey(a.ip).localeCompare(ipKey(b.ip)));
  if (!hosts.length) {
    panel.innerHTML = emptyBox(running ? "⏳" : "🔍", running ? "Scanning… devices will appear here as they are found." : "No devices responded.");
    return;
  }
  panel.innerHTML = `<div class="hosts">${hosts.map((h) => {
    const op = openPorts(h);
    const tags = [
      `<span class="tag ${op.length ? "open" : ""}">${op.length} open port${op.length === 1 ? "" : "s"}</span>`,
      h.vendor ? `<span class="tag">${esc(h.vendor)}</span>` : "",
      h.os ? `<span class="tag">${esc(h.os.name)} (${esc(h.os.accuracy)}%)</span>` : "",
    ].join("");
    const isOpen = expanded.has(h.ip) || hosts.length === 1;
    return `<div class="card host ${isOpen ? "expanded" : ""}" data-ip="${esc(h.ip)}">
      <div class="host-head"><span class="chev">▶</span>
        <span class="host-ip">${esc(h.ip)}</span>
        <span class="host-name">${esc(h.hostnames.join(", "))}</span>
        <div class="host-tags">${tags}</div></div>
      <div class="host-body">${hostDetails(h)}</div></div>`;
  }).join("")}</div>`;
}

function hostDetails(h) {
  const kv = [];
  if (h.hostnames?.length) kv.push(["Hostname", h.hostnames.join(", ")]);
  if (h.ipv6 && h.ipv6 !== h.ip) kv.push(["IPv6", h.ipv6]);
  if (h.mac) kv.push(["MAC", `${h.mac}${h.vendor ? ` (${h.vendor})` : ""}`]);
  if (h.os) kv.push(["OS guess", `${h.os.name} — ${h.os.accuracy}% sure`]);
  if (h.distance) kv.push(["Distance", `${h.distance} hop${h.distance > 1 ? "s" : ""}`]);
  if (h.reason) kv.push(["Detected by", h.reason]);
  for (const x of h.hidden_ports || []) kv.push(["Not shown", `${x.count} ${x.state} ports`]);
  if (h.trace?.length) kv.push(["Route", h.trace.map((t) => t.ip).join(" → ")]);
  let html = kv.length ? `<dl class="kv">${kv.map(([k, v]) => `<dt>${esc(k)}</dt><dd>${esc(v)}</dd>`).join("")}</dl>` : "";
  if (h.ports.length) {
    html += `<div class="table-wrap"><table class="table"><thead><tr><th>Port</th><th>State</th><th>Service</th><th>Software / version</th></tr></thead><tbody>${
      h.ports.map((p) => `<tr><td class="mono">${p.port}/${esc(p.proto)}</td>
        <td><span class="state ${esc(p.state)}">${esc(p.state)}</span></td>
        <td>${esc(p.service)}</td>
        <td>${esc(p.product)}${p.scripts.map((sc) => `<div class="script-out"><b>${esc(sc.id)}</b>: ${esc(sc.output)}</div>`).join("")}</td></tr>`).join("")
    }</tbody></table></div>`;
  } else if (!h.live) {
    html += `<p class="muted small">No open ports listed for this device${h.hidden_ports?.length ? "" : " (port scan not part of this scan type)"}.</p>`;
  }
  for (const sc of h.scripts || []) html += `<div class="script-out"><b>${esc(sc.id)}</b>: ${esc(sc.output)}</div>`;
  return html;
}

function renderPorts(result) {
  const q = $("#portFilter").value.trim().toLowerCase();
  const rows = [];
  for (const h of result.hosts) for (const p of h.ports) {
    const text = `${h.ip} ${h.hostnames.join(" ")} ${p.port} ${p.proto} ${p.state} ${p.service} ${p.product}`.toLowerCase();
    if (!q || text.includes(q)) rows.push({ h, p });
  }
  rows.sort((a, b) => ipKey(a.h.ip).localeCompare(ipKey(b.h.ip)) || a.p.port - b.p.port);

function renderMap(result) {
  const svg = $("#map");
  const hosts = [...result.hosts].sort((a, b) => ipKey(a.ip).localeCompare(ipKey(b.ip)));
  if (!hosts.length) {
    svg.setAttribute("viewBox", "0 0 400 120");
    svg.innerHTML = `<text x="200" y="64" text-anchor="middle" fill="var(--muted)" font-size="14">No devices to draw yet.</text>`;
    return;
  }
  // Place devices on rings around the scanner: ring k holds up to 8*k nodes.
  const placed = [];
  let ring = 1, idx = 0;
  while (idx < hosts.length) {
    const cap = 8 * ring;
    const n = Math.min(cap, hosts.length - idx);
    const r = 140 + (ring - 1) * 100;
    const offset = ring % 2 ? 0 : Math.PI / n;
    for (let i = 0; i < n; i++) {
      const a = -Math.PI / 2 + offset + (2 * Math.PI * i) / n;
      placed.push({ h: hosts[idx + i], x: r * Math.cos(a), y: r * Math.sin(a) });
    }
    idx += n; ring++;
  }
  const R = 140 + (ring - 2) * 100 + 80;
  svg.setAttribute("viewBox", `${-R} ${-R} ${2 * R} ${2 * R}`);
  const showSub = hosts.length <= 40;
  const links = placed.map((p) => `<line class="link" x1="0" y1="0" x2="${p.x.toFixed(1)}" y2="${p.y.toFixed(1)}"/>`).join("");
  const nodes = placed.map(({ h, x, y }) => {
    const n = openPorts(h).length;
    const color = n === 0 ? "var(--node-none)" : n < 5 ? "var(--node-some)" : "var(--node-many)";
    const rad = 10 + Math.min(n, 10);
    const name = h.hostnames[0] || h.vendor || "";
    const tip = [h.ip, name, `${n} open port${n === 1 ? "" : "s"}`,
      ...openPorts(h).slice(0, 12).map((p) => `  ${p.port}/${p.proto} ${p.service}`)].filter(Boolean).join("\n");
    return `<g class="node" data-ip="${esc(h.ip)}" transform="translate(${x.toFixed(1)},${y.toFixed(1)})">
      <title>${esc(tip)}</title>
      <circle r="${rad}" fill="${color}"/>
      <text y="${rad + 14}" text-anchor="middle">${esc(h.ip)}</text>
      ${showSub && name ? `<text class="sub" y="${rad + 27}" text-anchor="middle">${esc(name.length > 22 ? name.slice(0, 21) + "…" : name)}</text>` : ""}
    </g>`;
  }).join("");
  const center = `<g class="node center"><circle r="34"/><text text-anchor="middle" y="-2">Scanner</text>
    <text class="sub" text-anchor="middle" y="13">${hosts.length} device${hosts.length === 1 ? "" : "s"}</text></g>`;
  svg.innerHTML = links + center + nodes;
}

function fmtTime(ts) {
  return new Date(ts * 1000).toLocaleString(undefined, {
    weekday: "short", day: "numeric", month: "short", year: "numeric", hour: "2-digit", minute: "2-digit",
  });
}

const plural = (n, word) => `${n} ${word}${n === 1 ? "" : "s"}`;
const hostLabel = (h) => h.ip + (h.hostnames?.length ? ` (${h.hostnames.join(", ")})` : "");
const portText = (p) => `${p.port}/${p.proto}${p.service ? " " + p.service : ""}${p.product ? ` (${p.product})` : ""}`;

function deviceDetails(h, portsScanned) {
  const bits = [];
  if (h.mac) bits.push(`MAC ${h.mac}${h.vendor ? ` (${h.vendor})` : ""}`);
  if (h.os) bits.push(`OS guess ${h.os.name}`);
  if (portsScanned) {
    const open = h.ports.filter((p) => p.state === "open");
    bits.push(open.length ? `open ports: ${open.map(portText).join(", ")}` : "no open ports found");
  }
  return bits;
}

function summaryLine(d) {
  const parts = [];
  if (d.new_hosts.length) parts.push(plural(d.new_hosts.length, "new device"));
  if (d.missing_hosts.length) parts.push(`${plural(d.missing_hosts.length, "device")} not seen`);
  if (d.port_changes.length) parts.push(`port changes on ${plural(d.port_changes.length, "device")}`);
  if (d.other_changes.length) parts.push(plural(d.other_changes.length, "other change"));
  return parts.length ? parts.join(", ") + "." : "No changes found.";
}

const totalChanges = (d) => d.new_hosts.length + d.missing_hosts.length + d.port_changes.length + d.other_changes.length;

function textList(items, fn) {
  return items.length ? items.map(fn).join("\n") : "  (none)";
}

function compareCandidates(cur) {
  const usable = state.scans.filter((s) => s.id !== cur.id && s.status !== "running" && s.hosts_up != null);
  const same = usable.filter((s) => s.target === cur.target);
  const other = usable.filter((s) => s.target !== cur.target);
  return { same, other };
}

function defaultCandidate(cur, same) {
  return same.find((s) => s.started < cur.started) || same[0] || null;
}

function mailtoLink(to, subject, body) {
  const addr = to.split(/[,;\s]+/).filter(Boolean).map(encodeURIComponent).join(",");
  const make = (b) => `mailto:${addr}?subject=${encodeURIComponent(subject)}&body=${encodeURIComponent(b)}`;
  if (make(body).length <= MAILTO_LIMIT) return { url: make(body), shortened: false };
  const tail = "\n\n[Shortened - the full list is in the attached report.]\n";
  const lines = body.split("\n");
  while (lines.length && make(lines.join("\n") + tail).length > MAILTO_LIMIT) lines.pop();
  return { url: make(lines.join("\n") + tail), shortened: true };
}

def port(n, service="", product="", state="open"):

def host(ip, ports=(), mac="", vendor="", names=()):
    return {"ip": ip, "ipv6": "", "mac": mac, "vendor": vendor, "hostnames": list(names), "reason": "",
            "ports": list(ports), "hidden_ports": [], "os": None, "distance": None, "trace": [], "scripts": []}


def make_scan(sid, started, hosts, preset="quick", status="done", target="192.168.1.0/24"):
    d = {"id": sid, "targets": target.split(), "target": target, "preset": preset, "status": status,
         "started": started, "finished": started + 10, "progress": 100, "log": [], "error": None,
         "result": {"hosts": hosts, "summary": "", "elapsed": 10, "hosts_up": len(hosts),
                    "hosts_down": 0, "hosts_total": 256}}
    path = server.DATA / f"{sid}.json"
    path.write_text(json.dumps(d))
    return server.Scan.load(path)

"""End-to-end smoke test: starts the server, scans 127.0.0.1, checks results.

Run:  python3 tests/test_smoke.py
Scan history goes to a temporary folder, so your real history is untouched.
"""
import http.client
import json
import os
import socket
import subprocess
import sys
import tempfile
import time
from pathlib import Path

ROOT = Path(__file__).resolve().parent.parent


def free_port():
    with socket.socket() as s:
        s.bind(("127.0.0.1", 0))
        return s.getsockname()[1]


def request(port, method, path, body=None, host=None, ctype="application/json"):
    c = http.client.HTTPConnection("127.0.0.1", port, timeout=30)
    headers = {"Host": host or f"127.0.0.1:{port}"}
    if body is not None:
        headers["Content-Type"] = ctype
    c.request(method, path, body=json.dumps(body) if body is not None else None, headers=headers)
    r = c.getresponse()
    data = r.read()
    try:
        data = json.loads(data)
    except ValueError:
        pass
    return r.status, data


def check(cond, msg):
    print(("  ok    " if cond else "  FAIL  ") + msg)
    if not cond:
        global failed
        failed = True


failed = False


def main():
    port = free_port()
    tmp = tempfile.mkdtemp(prefix="nmap-gui-test-")
    env = {**os.environ, "XDG_DATA_HOME": tmp}
    srv = subprocess.Popen([sys.executable, str(ROOT / "server.py"), "--port", str(port), "--no-browser"],
                           env=env, stdout=subprocess.DEVNULL, stderr=subprocess.PIPE)
    try:
        for _ in range(50):
            try:
                request(port, "GET", "/api/info")
                break
            except OSError:
                time.sleep(0.1)

        print("Pages")
        for path in ("/", "/app.js", "/app.css", "/icon.svg"):
            st, _ = request(port, "GET", path)
            check(st == 200, f"GET {path} -> {st}")

        print("Safety checks")
        st, _ = request(port, "GET", "/api/info", host="evil.example.com")
        check(st == 403, f"foreign Host header refused ({st})")
        st, _ = request(port, "POST", "/api/scans", {"target": "127.0.0.1", "preset": "quick"}, ctype="text/plain")
        check(st == 400, f"non-JSON post refused ({st})")
        for bad in ["-oN /tmp/x 127.0.0.1", "127.0.0.1;rm -rf ~", "$(id)", ""]:
            st, d = request(port, "POST", "/api/scans", {"target": bad, "preset": "quick"})
            check(st == 400, f"bad target {bad!r} refused: {d.get('error') if isinstance(d, dict) else d}")
        st, _ = request(port, "POST", "/api/scans", {"target": "127.0.0.1", "preset": "nope"})
        check(st == 400, "unknown preset refused")

        print("Info")
        st, info = request(port, "GET", "/api/info")
        check(st == 200 and info["nmap"], f"nmap detected: {info.get('nmap')}")

            except OSError:
                time.sleep(0.1)
        check(any(x["id"] == sid for x in lst), "old scan still there after restart")
    finally:
        srv.terminate()
        srv.wait()
        err = srv.stderr.read().decode()
        if err.strip():
            print("server stderr:\n" + err)

    print("\nRESULT:", "FAILED" if failed else "ALL PASSED")
    sys.exit(1 if failed else 0)


if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()

#!/usr/bin/env python3
# Copyright (C) 2026 Denis (https://github.com/agent-denis)
# SPDX-License-Identifier: GPL-3.0-or-later
"""Plain-text versions of the project for people who prefer (or need) .txt files.

    README.txt                 README.md converted to plain text (committed)
    nmap-gui-source.txt    every source file in one text file (committed; refresh it before a release)

Usage:
    python3 tools/export_text.py            # both
    python3 tools/export_text.py readme     # only README.txt
    python3 tools/export_text.py source     # only nmap-gui-source.txt
"""
import datetime
import re
import subprocess
import sys
import textwrap
from pathlib import Path

ROOT = Path(__file__).resolve().parent.parent
WIDTH = 80


# --------------------------------------------------------------------------- README.md -> README.txt
def _inline(text: str) -> str:
    text = re.sub(r"!\[([^\]]*)\]\(([^)]+)\)", r"[Screenshot: \1 – \2]", text)
    text = re.sub(r"\[([^\]]+)\]\(#[^)]*\)", r"\1", text)                       # links inside the README
    text = re.sub(r"\[`?([^\]`]+)`?\]\(([^)]+)\)",
                  lambda m: m.group(1) if m.group(1) == m.group(2) else f"{m.group(1)} ({m.group(2)})", text)
    text = re.sub(r"\*\*([^*]+)\*\*", r"\1", text)
    text = re.sub(r"(?<![\w*])\*([^*\n]+)\*(?![\w*])", r"\1", text)
    text = re.sub(r"`([^`]+)`", r"\1", text)
    return text


def _table(rows: list[str]) -> list[str]:
    cells = [[_inline(c.strip()) for c in r.strip().strip("|").split("|")] for r in rows]
    cells = [r for r in cells if not all(re.fullmatch(r":?-+:?", c) for c in r)]
    head, body = cells[0], cells[1:]
    widths = [max(len(r[i]) for r in cells if i < len(r)) for i in range(len(head))]
    if sum(widths) + 3 * len(widths) <= WIDTH:
        line = lambda r: "  " + "   ".join(c.ljust(w) for c, w in zip(r, widths)).rstrip()
        return [line(head), "  " + "   ".join("-" * w for w in widths)] + [line(r) for r in body]
    out = []                                          # too wide for columns: one block per row
    for r in body:
        extra = "; ".join(f"{h.lower()}: {c}" for h, c in zip(head[2:], r[2:]) if c)
        text = f"{r[0]}: {r[1]}" + (f" ({extra})" if extra else "")
        out += textwrap.wrap(text, WIDTH, initial_indent="  - ", subsequent_indent="    ")
    return out


def readme_text(md: str) -> str:
    head, md = md.split("\n", 1)
    title = head.lstrip("# ").strip()
    intro, md = md.strip("\n").split("\n\n", 1)
    out = [title.upper(), "=" * len(title), ""] + textwrap.wrap(_inline(" ".join(intro.split())), WIDTH) + [""]
    lines = md.splitlines()
    i = 0
    para: list[str] = []

    def flush():
        if para:
            text = _inline(" ".join(s.strip() for s in para))
            m = re.match(r"^(\s*)(- |\d+\. )(.*)", para[0])
            if m:
                ind = " " * (2 + len(m.group(2)))
                out.extend(textwrap.wrap(_inline(" ".join([m.group(3)] + [s.strip() for s in para[1:]])), WIDTH,
                                         initial_indent="  " + m.group(2), subsequent_indent=ind))
            else:
                out.extend(textwrap.wrap(text, WIDTH))
            para.clear()

    while i < len(lines):
        line = lines[i]
        if line.startswith("```"):
            flush()
            i += 1
            while i < len(lines) and not lines[i].startswith("```"):
                out.append("    " + lines[i])
                i += 1
            out.append("")
        elif line.startswith("## Contents"):
            flush()
            i += 1
            while i < len(lines) and not lines[i].startswith("## "):   # the link list only makes sense on GitHub
                i += 1
            continue
        elif line.startswith("## "):
            flush()
            head = _inline(line[3:]).upper()
            out += ["", head, "-" * len(head), ""]
        elif line.startswith("### "):
            flush()
            head = _inline(line[4:])
            out += [head, "~" * len(head), ""]
        elif line.startswith("|"):
            flush()
            rows = []
            while i < len(lines) and lines[i].startswith("|"):
                rows.append(lines[i])
                i += 1
            out += _table(rows) + [""]
            continue
        elif line.startswith(">"):
            flush()
            out.extend(textwrap.wrap(_inline(line.lstrip("> ")), WIDTH, initial_indent="  | ", subsequent_indent="  | "))
        elif not line.strip():
            flush()
            if out and out[-1] != "":
                out.append("")
        elif re.match(r"^\s*(- |\d+\. )", line):
            flush()
            para.append(line)
        else:
            para.append(line)
        i += 1
    flush()
    text = "\n".join(out)
    text = re.sub(r"\n{3,}", "\n\n", text).strip() + "\n"
    return text + "\n(Plain-text version of README.md, made by tools/export_text.py.)\n"


def write_readme():
    (ROOT / "README.txt").write_text(readme_text((ROOT / "README.md").read_text(encoding="utf-8")), encoding="utf-8")
    print("wrote README.txt")


# --------------------------------------------------------------------------- source code export
SKIP = re.compile(r"^(docs/screenshots/|Screenshots/|LICENSE$|README\.txt$)|\.(png|svg)$")


def write_source():
    files = subprocess.run(["git", "ls-files", "-co", "--exclude-standard"], cwd=ROOT, capture_output=True,
                           text=True, check=True).stdout.split()
    files = sorted(f for f in files if not SKIP.search(f) and f != "nmap-gui-source.txt" and (ROOT / f).is_file())
    texts = {f: (ROOT / f).read_text(encoding="utf-8") for f in files}
    count = {f: len(t.splitlines()) for f, t in texts.items()}
    version = re.search(r'__version__ = "(.*)"', texts["server.py"]).group(1)
    out = ["NMAP-GUI – SOURCE CODE",
           f"Version {version} · exported {datetime.datetime.now():%Y-%m-%d %H:%M}",
           "Licence: GNU General Public License v3.0 or later (the full text is in LICENSE in the repository)",
           "Homepage: https://github.com/agent-denis/nmap-gui",
           f"{len(files)} files, {sum(count.values())} lines. Not included: the LICENSE text, screenshots and icons.",
           "", "FILES"]
    out += [f"  {f:<64}{count[f]:>7} lines" for f in files]
    out += ["", ""]
    bar = "=" * 100
    for f in files:
        out += [bar, f"FILE: {f}   ({count[f]} lines)", bar, texts[f].rstrip("\n"), "", ""]
    (ROOT / "nmap-gui-source.txt").write_text("\n".join(out) + "\n", encoding="utf-8")
    print(f"wrote nmap-gui-source.txt ({len(files)} files, {sum(count.values())} lines)")


if __name__ == "__main__":
    what = sys.argv[1] if len(sys.argv) > 1 else "all"
    if what in ("all", "readme"):
        write_readme()
    if what in ("all", "source"):
        write_source()

#!/usr/bin/env bash

# Remove nmap-gui installed by install.sh.
#
#   ./uninstall.sh            remove the program, command and menu entry (keeps scan history and settings)
#   ./uninstall.sh --purge    also delete scan history and settings
set -euo pipefail

PURGE=0
[ "${1:-}" = "--purge" ] && PURGE=1

DATA="${XDG_DATA_HOME:-$HOME/.local/share}"
case "$DATA" in /*) ;; *) DATA="$HOME/.local/share" ;; esac
BASE="$DATA/nmap-gui"
MANIFEST="$BASE/installed-files.txt"

# stop it if it is running
command -v nmap-gui >/dev/null 2>&1 && nmap-gui --stop >/dev/null 2>&1 || true

if [ -f "$MANIFEST" ]; then
    while IFS= read -r f; do
        # only remove files inside the home folder that install.sh put there
        case "$f" in "$HOME"/*) rm -f -- "$f" ;; esac
    done < "$MANIFEST"
    rm -f "$MANIFEST"
fi
rm -rf "$BASE/app" "$BASE/app.new"
touch "$DATA/icons/hicolor" 2>/dev/null || true
command -v update-desktop-database >/dev/null 2>&1 && update-desktop-database -q "$DATA/applications" 2>/dev/null || true

if [ "$PURGE" -eq 1 ]; then
    rm -rf "$BASE"
    echo "nmap-gui removed, including scan history and settings."
else
    rmdir "$BASE" 2>/dev/null || true
    echo "nmap-gui removed. Scan history and settings are kept in $BASE"
    echo "(delete them with: ./uninstall.sh --purge)"
fi
